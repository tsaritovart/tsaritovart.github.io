# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Assumption for this build: static HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript without a framework or build step, compatible with GitHub Pages.

## Users

Primary users are art directors, creative directors, design leads, recruiters, and potential clients evaluating Arthur Tsaritov's creative direction, AI craft, web work, and traditional design practice.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Arthur Tsaritov as an AI Creative & Art Director. Success means a visitor can immediately understand the practice, experience the work at useful scale, explore a live web project, and contact Arthur.

## Positioning

The portfolio connects generative moving-image work, interactive web direction, and traditional commercial design as one creative-directing practice rather than three unrelated galleries.

## Operating Context

The site is viewed as a standalone creative artifact on desktop and mobile. Visitors typically scan quickly, then linger on motion, live web work, and selected cases.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One responsive portfolio surface with three work categories.
- AI work appears as a moving gallery using the supplied MP4 files.
- The Goulash web project must be scrollable live inside the portfolio and also open directly.
- Traditional design contains three supplied cases: Oral-B, Alfa, and Rowenta.
- No React, framework, or build tool is assumed for this build.
- Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and remain usable without autoplay.
- Real project assets must not be replaced with invented stock or generated proof.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Arthur Tsaritov.
- Role line: AI Creative & Art Director.
- Explicit direction: modern, futuristic, dark, and fluid.
- The prior portfolio concept and visual system are not authoritative.

## Evidence on Hand

- Seven AI MP4 works in `AI/`.
- A complete static Goulash web project in `web/gulash/`.
- Three-image case sets for Oral-B, Alfa, and Rowenta in `traditional/`.
- No supplied metrics, testimonials, awards, or detailed case-study claims; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads; interface chrome recedes.
2. Motion is shown as motion, not reduced to static thumbnails.
3. Web work remains interactive and explorable.
4. Traditional design is presented with enough scale and sequence to judge craft.
5. Every claim is supported by a supplied artifact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must preserve keyboard access, readable contrast, semantic navigation, responsive behavior, and a reduced-motion alternative.
